### A. Agniveers: two-year second engagement approved
- On 4 November, the Union Cabinet approved a further two-year engagement period for the 75% of Agniveers not selected for regular enrolment after their initial four-year term.
- The first 25% continue to be offered regular employment on merit. After the additional two-year period, willing Agniveers may be offered enrolment as regular soldiers, subject to selection criteria and willingness.

- **Editorial nuance:** Do not say all Agniveers automatically become permanent soldiers. Final enrolment remains conditional.
- **Why it matters:** A significant change for the first batch approaching the end of its original tenure; high relevance for defence aspirants and families.

### B. ₹2,814-crore critical-minerals processing scheme
- The Union Cabinet approved a five-year, ₹2,814-crore capex support scheme for domestic processing of critical minerals, especially lithium and nickel.
- The scheme period is FY 2026–27 to FY 2030–31. It provides a 30% capital subsidy for four proposed plants: two lithium plants (minimum 30 kt each) and two nickel plants (minimum 50 kt each).
- Stated maximum support caps are ₹500 crore per lithium beneficiary and ₹900 crore per nickel beneficiary.
- **Editorial nuance:** Explain that this is a capacity-building scheme, not proof that India has already become self-sufficient in lithium or nickel processing. Cobalt may be recovered as a by-product of nickel processing.
- **Why it matters:** EV batteries, electronics, energy storage and defence supply chains.

### C. Aditya-L1 reveals asymmetric expansion of a solar eruption
- A study reported on 3 November used Aditya-L1's VELC observations, NASA's Solar Dynamics Observatory data and 3-D modelling to reconstruct an eruption observed on 5 August 2024.
- The eruption expanded asymmetrically rather than as a simple symmetric bubble. This can improve understanding of coronal mass ejections and the uncertainty around their speed and arrival-time forecasts.
- **Editorial nuance:** This is a research finding about space-weather modelling. Do not imply that a solar storm is currently heading for Earth or that a satellite disruption has occurred.
- **Why it matters:** A compelling Indian science/space story with relevance to satellites and communication systems.
