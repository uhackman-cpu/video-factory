### W2 — Western UP food-safety action: Bareilly, Moradabad and Muzaffarnagar
**Allocation:** 115 seconds, Western UP.  
Reports quoting UP FSDA say the 26 Oct–3 Nov campaign inspected 1,133 locations, collected 935 samples, seized about 54 quintals of food worth around ₹1.32 crore and suspended 27 licences. Reports describe 32 mobile squads in Bareilly and Moradabad, with hygiene/ingredient issues found in some inspections. About 220 kg of sweets were reportedly destroyed in Muzaffarnagar after flies/insects were found; reports differ on the sweet's exact name, so the script says only “sweets”. A seizure or hygiene finding is not the same as a lab-confirmed adulteration result. Do not name or shame a shop.

**Allocation:** 94 seconds, India.

### N2 — FSSAI energy-drink labelling dispute
**Allocation:** 60 seconds, India.  
FSSAI's court filings say the “energy drink” descriptor on certain high-caffeine beverages may mislead consumers. PepsiCo and Monster dispute the regulator's process. Earlier interim relief allowed sale of stock manufactured up to 6 October, but not new manufacture with the disputed label. A hearing is listed for 5 November; as of 02:18 IST no hearing result was confirmed. This is a labelling/legal dispute, not a court finding that the beverages are poisonous.

### N3 — CERT-In critical advisory on AI-assisted financial cyberattacks
**Allocation:** 70 seconds, India.  
CERT-In advisory CIAD-2026-0058, issued 4 November, describes an active campaign by “CRAFTY FALCON” targeting banks, NBFCs, fintechs, digital lenders, payment aggregators and related financial entities. It describes AI-assisted attack tooling, exposed non-production systems, API abuse and unauthorised fund-transfer attempts. This does not mean every customer's account is compromised. Consumer advice is limited to safe, general precautions: never share OTP/UPI PIN, verify beneficiary and amount, avoid unknown links and report financial fraud quickly.  
Sources: https://www.cert-in.org.in/s2cMainServlet?VLCODE=CIAD-2026-0058&pageid=PUBVLNOTES02 ; https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/india-cybercrime-agency-warns-financial-firms-of-ai-attacks-2026-11-04/ ; https://www.livemint.com/technology/central-cyber-security-agency-warns-banks-fintech-firms-of-frauds-through-ai-agents-11793805354383.html ; https://cybercrime.gov.in/

Barium/non-approved and series crackers are not allowed. Human review required for the fireworks visual.
